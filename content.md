## Featured Projects

### Driver Fatigue Detection and Alert System

**Period:** 2025–2026  
**Role:** ML Lead  
**Focus:** Computer vision · Mobile ML · Hardware integration

A hybrid ESP32–Android system designed to detect signs of driver fatigue and provide alerts. A dashboard-mounted ESP32-S3-N16R8 with an OV2640 camera streams live video to a companion Android application for real-time analysis.

**My contribution**

- Served as ML Lead within the development team.
- Trained a custom TensorFlow Lite model to track facial landmarks.
- Integrated the model into the Android application to compute Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR).
- Implemented fatigue-triggered visual and audio alerts, along with an ESP32-activated buzzer.

**Technologies:** TensorFlow Lite, Android, ESP32-S3-N16R8, OV2640 camera

**Project highlight:** Connects model training, mobile inference, and hardware alerts in a two-node hybrid edge-computing system.
